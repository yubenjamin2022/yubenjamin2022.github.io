Hi! I'm Ben, a master's student at UCSD, majoring in Computer Science. I am currently a Research Fellow with SPAR, advised by Dr. Christopher Ackerman, where I study introspection & self-awareness. I am also a researcher with the PLUS Lab of the UCLA NLP Group, advised by Wenbo Hu and Professor Violet Peng. Here, I am researching introspection and AI Safety of language models and agents.

At a philosophical level, I am interested in understanding the fundamental principles underlying intelligence: what it means for a being to be intelligent, how intelligence arises, and what makes it possible. To that end, I view artificial intelligence as a means of investigating these questions. Through my research, I hope to uncover principles of intelligence that explain both natural and artificial intelligence.

I'm always looking for new and interesting research opportunities in these areas, so feel free to reach out if you'd like to collaborate!
